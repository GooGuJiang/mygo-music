The native Demucs worker uses audio.cpp and ggml.
https://github.com/0xShug0/audio.cpp/releases/tag/v0.9.5
See LICENSE.audio.cpp in each backend directory.

The four-stem HTDemucs 955717e8 model comes from Demucs.
https://github.com/adefossez/demucs
The GGUF was published by audio.cpp. MyGo adjusts its inference segment metadata.
https://huggingface.co/audio-cpp/audio.cpp-gguf/tree/33120a4cfdf0d56786db934a03a093d4675de938/HTDemucs-GGUF

The CUDA Runtime, cuBLAS, and cuFFT libraries are NVIDIA components.
https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/archive/13.3.0/eula/index.html

Beat This! by Francesco Foscarin, Jan Schlüter and Gerhard Widmer, final0 model
exported to ONNX by TunePerfect. See LICENSE.beat-this.
https://github.com/CPJKU/beat_this
https://huggingface.co/tuneperfect/beat-this-onnx

ONNX Runtime and NVIDIA cuDNN run the rhythm model on the Demucs CUDA device.
See LICENSE.onnxruntime, THIRD-PARTY.onnxruntime.txt and LICENSE.cudnn.
https://onnxruntime.ai/
https://developer.nvidia.com/cudnn
